About

Academic Profile

Prof. Ranganath M. Singari is a distinguished Professor at Delhi Technological University (formerly Delhi College of Engineering), where he has served since 1998. He holds a dual appointment in the Department of Mechanical, Production & Industrial Engineering and the Department of Design.


A visionary academic leader, he founded and headed the Department of Design at DTU from 2018 to 2023 — establishing it as one of India's premier design education programs. He currently chairs three major centres at DTU: CIDE (Centre for Industrial Design & Ergonomics), CAPIER (Centre for Advanced Production and Industrial Engineering Research), and DTU ALTAIR (Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics).


His research spans production engineering, optimization techniques, industrial design, friction stir welding, additive manufacturing, and the emerging intersection of AI and design. He has published over 200 research papers, guided 12+ PhD scholars, mentored 85+ B.Tech and 18+ M.Tech projects, and organized prestigious international conferences including the ICAPIE series published by Springer.

Research Focus Areas

Production & Manufacturing Engineering

CNC machining optimization, surface roughness analysis, and advanced manufacturing processes using Taguchi, RSM, and ANOVA approaches.

🔗

Friction Stir Welding & Processing

Joining dissimilar metals and alloys, surface composite fabrication, and mechanical property enhancement of welded joints.

🎨

Industrial & Product Design

Human-centered design, ergonomics, user experience, and the integration of engineering and design principles in innovation.

🖨️

Additive Manufacturing

Impact of 3D printing on supply chains, nanocomposite fabrication, and advanced material processing for functional applications.

🧠

AI & Design Innovation

Exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence and design methodologies for next-generation product development and creative processes.

🎭

Color Psychology & Neurodesign

Influence of color on visual psychology, cognitive behavior, and decision-making in healthcare and educational environments.

🏥

Healthcare Design

Diagnostic device design, pediatric hospital environments, sustainable transport frameworks, and ergonomic interventions in clinical settings.

🛡️

Surface Coatings & Composites

HVOF sprayed functionally graded coatings, MWCNT reinforced nanocomposites, and surface texturing of CNC tool inserts.

📊

Optimization Techniques

Multi-criteria decision making, Taguchi methods, response surface methodology, and ANOVA for manufacturing parameter optimization.
