About

Academic Profile

Prof. Ranganath M. Singari is a distinguished Professor at Delhi Technological University (formerly Delhi College of Engineering), where he has served since 1998. He holds a dual appointment in the Department of Mechanical, Production & Industrial Engineering and the Department of Design.


A visionary academic leader, he founded and headed the Department of Design at DTU from 2018 to 2023 — establishing it as one of India's premier design education programs. He currently chairs three major centres at DTU: CIDE, CAPIER, and DTU ALTAIR.


His research spans production engineering, optimization techniques, industrial design, friction stir welding, additive manufacturing, and the intersection of AI and design. He has published over 200 research papers, guided 12+ PhD scholars, and organized prestigious international conferences including the ICAPIE series published by Springer.

🔬 Centre of Excellence

CAPIER DTU

The Centre for Advanced Production and Industrial Engineering Research (CAPIER) was established on 8 April 2015 at Delhi Technological University. Under the leadership of Prof. Ranganath M. Singari as Principal Investigator, CAPIER has become a globally recognized hub for manufacturing research, industry-academia collaboration, and the prestigious ICAPIE international conference series.
